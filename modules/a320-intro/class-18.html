CLASS 18 · ATA 35

Oxygen

El sistema de oxígeno no repara una despresurización ni elimina el humo. Proporciona una atmósfera respirable temporal mientras la tripulación recupera un entorno supervivible, desciende a una altitud segura o gestiona una cabina contaminada.

09 · PORTABLE OXYGEN vs PBE

Not every oxygen-related device solves the same problem.

Selecciona el escenario. La herramienta correcta depende de si el objetivo es proporcionar oxygen supply o proteger a una persona que debe trabajar dentro de smoke/fumes.

PORTABLE OXYGEN

Supplemental/mobile oxygen supply. Exact bottle type, masks and use follow operator equipment.

PBE

Protective breathing equipment for smoke/fumes exposure during cabin tasks. Closed-circuit design and endurance depend on the installed PBE model.

Scenario: a passenger needs supplemental oxygen. Portable oxygen is the relevant resource; PBE is not a passenger-treatment device.
10 · OXYGEN + FIRE HAZARD

Oxygen does not burn. It makes combustion more intense.

El peligro operacional es el enriquecimiento de oxígeno alrededor de materiales combustibles y una fuente de ignición.

OXYGEN / OXIDIZER
COMBUSTIBLE MATERIAL
IGNITION
NO COMBUSTION CONDITION
Normal: oxygen is an oxidizer, not a fuel. Handling oxygen requires strict control of contamination, ignition sources and incompatible materials.
11 · FAILURE REASONING

Ask what breathable capability remains.

Un fallo puede afectar presión, deployment, one mask group or a specific crew position. La consecuencia se entiende mejor por capacidad restante que por el nombre del componente.

12 · SYSTEM CONNECTIONS

Oxygen is not an isolated ATA chapter.

La utilidad real aparece cuando el alumno conecta oxygen con pressurization, ECAM, communications and fire/smoke response.

CLASS 7 · PRESSURIZATION

Cabin altitude and decompression create the oxygen requirement. Oxygen does not repair the pressurization failure.

CLASS 14 · EIS / ECAM

Oxygen pressure and related alerts are interpreted through the aircraft display/alerting architecture.

CLASS 16 · COMMUNICATIONS

With crew masks donned, microphone/audio routing and crew confirmation become part of the immediate response.

CLASS 17 · FIRE PROTECTION

PBE and crew oxygen masks support different roles during smoke/fire events. They are not interchangeable tools.
